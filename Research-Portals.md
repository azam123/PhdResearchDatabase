# 🌐 Research, Topic and PhD Opportunity Portals

> Starting points for discovering actual research projects, doctoral vacancies, publications, and funding. Always cross-check an opportunity on the institution's official website.

## Multidisciplinary discovery

| Portal | Coverage | Use |
|---|---|---|
| [FindAPhD](https://www.findaphd.com/phds/) | UK, Europe, international | Search by subject, country, institution, supervisor, and funding |
| [Nature Index Topic Explorer](https://www.nature.com/nature-index/topics/topic-explorer) | Global research | Explore active research themes across applied, biological, chemical, earth, health, physical, and social sciences |
| [OpenAlex](https://openalex.org/) | Global scholarly literature | Discover papers, authors, institutions, concepts, and citation relationships |
| [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/) | Global scholarly literature | Find recent papers, authors, and citation trails |
| [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/) | Open-access journals | Locate open-access research articles across disciplines |
| [BASE](https://www.base-search.net/) | Academic web resources | Search repositories, theses, and scholarly documents |

## Country-oriented starting points

### United States

- [NSF](https://www.nsf.gov/) — science and engineering research funding
- [NIH](https://www.nih.gov/) — biomedical and health research
- [DOE Office of Science](https://science.osti.gov/) — energy and physical sciences
- [USDA NIFA](https://www.nifa.usda.gov/) — agriculture and food systems research

### United Kingdom

- [UKRI](https://www.ukri.org/) — research councils and funding opportunities
- [Find a PhD](https://www.findaphd.com/phds/) — doctoral projects and studentships
- [Jobs.ac.uk](https://www.jobs.ac.uk/) — academic vacancies and research roles

### Switzerland

- [swissuniversities](https://www.swissuniversities.ch/en/) — Swiss higher-education information

- [ETH Zurich doctoral studies](https://ethz.ch/en/doctorate.html) — doctoral information
- [EPFL doctoral school](https://www.epfl.ch/education/phd/) — doctoral programmes and requirements

## How to use this directory

1. Identify a research question and 5–10 keywords.
2. Search recent papers from the last 3–5 years.
3. Find research groups working on the question.
4. Open the official university topic, lab, doctoral, or vacancy page.
5. Verify supervisor availability and funding before contacting anyone.
6. Save the official URL, access date, and status in the database.

**Verification note:** Portal listings are discovery aids; they are not proof that a specific supervisor is accepting students or that funding is currently available.
