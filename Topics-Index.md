## Topic record schema

Every detailed topic record should include:

- Specific proposed title and problem statement
- Research questions and expected contribution
- Methods, datasets, evaluation plan, and limitations
- Current evidence and recent papers
- Official university research-topic or project link
- Relevant research group and potential supervisor
- Public institutional contact details, if officially published
- Funding and application links
- Risks, ethics, and reproducibility considerations
- `status` and `last_verified`

## Status meanings

- `verified`: source checked and claim supported
- `needs-review`: promising lead requiring additional confirmation
- `expired`: opportunity or link is no longer current
- `unconfirmed`: not enough evidence to publish as a factual claim
