# 💰 PhD Funding Directory

## Funding categories

| Category | Examples to investigate | Verification requirement |
|---|---|---|
| University funding | Doctoral scholarships, assistantships | Official university page |
| Government funding | National research councils and ministries | Official government page |
| Research grants | Project-funded PhD positions | Official project or lab page |
| External scholarships | International scholarship programs | Official provider page |

## Record checklist

- Funding name and provider
- Eligible countries and applicant profiles
- Tuition coverage
- Stipend and benefits
- Deadline and duration
- Supervisor or admission requirements
- Official application URL
- `last_verified` date

Funding information changes frequently. Never treat a historical deadline as current.
