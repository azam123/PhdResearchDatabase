The database separates **research relevance** from general university rankings. A university listed for a topic is not automatically the best institution overall.

## 🏷️ Record conventions

Each record should use YAML front matter with an ID, type, country, status, last verification date, and source links. Information may be marked `verified`, `needs-review`, `expired`, or `unconfirmed`.

## ⚠️ Research integrity

- Do not present drafts as peer-reviewed publications.
- Use official university, laboratory, funding-body, and publisher links where possible.
- Record access dates and preserve uncertainty.
- Avoid copying copyrighted paper content; summarize and cite instead.
