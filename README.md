## 🧪 Subject coverage

The database is **not limited to Computer Science or AI**. It includes engineering, natural sciences, medicine and public health, agriculture, climate and environment, economics, business, education, psychology, law, public policy, sociology, humanities, languages, architecture, mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, and interdisciplinary research.

## 🔎 What each research record should contain

- Specific proposed research topic/title
- Subject, discipline, keywords, and interdisciplinary links
- University and department/institute
- Official university research-topic or doctoral-opportunity URL
- Potential supervisor and official faculty profile
- Public institutional contact details, only when published officially
- Relevant recent publications, projects, and research groups
- Funding and eligibility information
- Verification date, source quality, and confidence status
- Clear distinction between an advertised project and a proposed topic

## ⚠️ Research integrity

A professor listed in this repository is a **potential supervisor**, not a confirmed guide unless an official opportunity explicitly names them as supervisor. Supervision, admission, project availability, eligibility, and funding must be confirmed directly with the institution or professor.

The database should prefer official university, research institute, government funding, publisher, and laboratory sources. Do not invent contact details, active vacancies, funding amounts, or supervisor availability.

## 🔄 Maintenance model

Each record should be reviewed periodically, especially:

- Live PhD vacancies and deadlines
- Faculty affiliations and contact details
- Funding calls and eligibility rules
- Research-topic pages and project availability
- Broken or redirected URLs

See [RESEARCH-DATABASE-GUIDELINES.md](./RESEARCH-DATABASE-GUIDELINES.md) for the verification workflow.
