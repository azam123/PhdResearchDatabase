# 🧩 Research Gaps Register

Research gaps listed here are hypotheses for investigation, not established novelty claims.

| Area | Candidate gap | Validation method |
|---|---|---|
| Agentic AI | Reliable failure detection | Systematic review and benchmark comparison |
| RAG | Robustness to noisy or conflicting sources | Controlled retrieval experiments |
| AI security | Secure tool permissions and execution | Threat modeling and red-team evaluation |
| AI systems | Cost-aware quality optimization | Latency, cost, and quality benchmarking |
| Software engineering | Reproducible measurement of AI coding assistants | Replicated empirical study |

Each gap should be connected to papers, methods, datasets, and explicit evidence of what remains unresolved.
