## 🔎 Discovery rules

1. Start with a subject and research question, not only a university name.
2. Check the university's official research group, doctoral programme, and vacancy page.
3. Verify supervisor affiliation and public institutional contact details.
4. Record the date on which the information was checked.
5. Treat an advertised project and a self-designed proposal as different record types.
