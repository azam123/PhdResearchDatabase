# 🤖 Reliable Agentic AI Systems

## Overview

Research on designing autonomous AI agents that are reliable, observable, secure, scalable, and measurable in real-world environments.

## Research questions

1. How can agent planning and tool use be evaluated reproducibly?
2. How should agents recover from incorrect actions and unreliable tools?
3. How can long-running agent workflows maintain security and auditability?
4. Which evaluation metrics correlate with real-world task success?

## Research directions

- Agent evaluation and benchmarking
- Planning, memory, and reflection
- Human oversight and escalation
- Secure tool execution
- Cost, latency, and token optimization
- Multi-agent coordination

## Research gaps to investigate

- Standardized evaluation across domains
- Reliable failure detection and recovery
- Reproducible measurement of self-improvement
- Security boundaries for tool-enabled agents

These are candidate research gaps and must be validated through a systematic literature review.
