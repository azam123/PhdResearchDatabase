# 👨‍🏫 Professors and Potential Supervisors

This index helps PhD aspirants identify **potential supervisors** using information published on official university, department, institute, and research-group websites.

> **Important:** A professor listed here is a potential research match, not a confirmed supervisor. A faculty profile does not prove that the professor is currently accepting PhD students or has available funding.

## Preferred information sources

Use sources in this order whenever possible:

1. Official university faculty profile
2. Official department or school profile
3. Official laboratory, institute, or research-group page
4. Official university doctoral programme or graduate admissions page
5. Official university funding, studentship, scholarship, or project page
6. Official publication profile or publisher page for publication verification

Avoid relying solely on commercial ranking websites, scraped directories, social media, or unverified aggregator pages.

## Recommended professor record

```yaml
id: unique-professor-record-id
name: Full name
academic_title: Professor|Associate Professor|Assistant Professor|Researcher|Other
university: Official university name
country: Country
department: Department, school, or institute
research_group: Laboratory, centre, or research group
research_interests:
  - Research area 1
  - Research area 2
  - Research area 3
relevant_topics:
  - Topic record ID
official_profile_url: Official university profile URL
research_group_url: Official research group URL, if available
phd_programme_url: Official doctoral programme URL, if available
public_institutional_email: Email only if publicly listed on an official source
supervision_information: Officially stated information only
funding_information: Officially stated information only
recent_publications:
  - Title, year, and official publication URL
source_urls:
  - URL supporting each important claim
status: verified|needs-review|unconfirmed|inactive
last_verified: YYYY-MM-DD
notes: Limitations, uncertainty, or application guidance
```

## Verification checklist

Before adding a professor record:

- [ ] Confirm the current university affiliation on an official university website.
- [ ] Confirm the department, institute, or research group.
- [ ] Extract research interests from the official profile rather than guessing from a single paper.
- [ ] Use official publication pages, ORCID, institutional repositories, or publisher pages to verify recent work.
- [ ] Include an official institutional email only when publicly displayed.
- [ ] Check whether the university officially mentions PhD supervision, open positions, funded projects, or studentships.
- [ ] Do not infer funding availability from a professor's research interests.
- [ ] Do not infer that a professor is accepting applications unless an official source states it.
- [ ] Record the exact date the profile and key information were checked.
- [ ] Mark outdated, unavailable, conflicting, or unclear information as `needs-review`.

## Useful application guidance for aspirants

Each record should help applicants answer:

1. Does my proposed topic align with this professor's published interests?
2. Is the professor affiliated with the relevant department or research centre?
3. Does the university offer a suitable PhD programme?
4. Is there an officially published funded project, studentship, or scholarship?
5. What are the programme eligibility requirements and deadlines?
6. Does the university require contacting a supervisor before applying?
7. What information should be included in an initial research-interest email?

## Contact ethics and privacy

- Use only publicly available professional contact information.
- Never guess email addresses from naming conventions.
- Do not collect private phone numbers, personal addresses, or sensitive personal information.
- Encourage applicants to follow the university's stated contact and admissions process.
- Avoid sending mass or irrelevant emails to faculty members.

## Verification status definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `verified` | Official sources were checked and the listed claims are supported. |
| `needs-review` | Some information is outdated, incomplete, conflicting, or inaccessible. |
| `unconfirmed` | The record needs stronger official-source evidence. |
| `inactive` | The person is no longer listed in the relevant role or the record is no longer applicable. |

`verified` does not mean admission, funding, or supervision is guaranteed.
