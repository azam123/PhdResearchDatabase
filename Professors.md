# 👨‍🏫 Professors and Research Groups

This index should contain only verified researchers whose current interests align with a topic record.

## Recommended fields

- Name and official profile URL
- University, department, and laboratory
- Research interests
- Recent relevant publications
- Supervision status, if officially stated
- Contact and application guidance
- `last_verified` date

> Do not infer supervision availability or funding from a profile alone. Verify through the official group or doctoral admissions page.
