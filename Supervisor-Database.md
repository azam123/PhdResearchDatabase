## Research-gap methodology

Research gaps in this database are **curated research directions**, not claims that a professor has explicitly endorsed a specific thesis. Each gap should be grounded in the faculty member's official research description, active programme/project information, and recent literature where available.

## Source and integrity rules

- Official university pages are the primary source for affiliation, research interests, recruitment and institutional contact information.
- Never infer an email address.
- Never treat a faculty profile as proof of an open funded PhD position.
- Historical deadlines are explicitly marked as historical.
- Potential supervisors are not guaranteed supervisors.
- Publication claims are separated from research-interest claims and should be independently verified when a paper title is added.
