## 2. University of Tübingen — PhD: Human-Centered Agentic Science

- **Institution:** University of Tübingen, Ludwig Uhland Institute for Historical and Cultural Anthropology, Germany
- **Discipline:** Cultural anthropology; digital anthropology; Science and Technology Studies (STS); sociology/media studies; human–technology relations; interdisciplinary AI-and-science studies
- **Research topic:** Ethnographic study of AI-based systems and the automation of scientific workflows within the interdisciplinary project “Human-Centered Agentic Science.” The project examines how ethnographic perspectives can contribute to development and implementation of agentic science while critically reflecting on automation of scientific work.
- **Official opportunity / research page:** https://uni-tuebingen.de/en/128980/
- **Position:** Doctoral position, E13 TV-L, 75%
- **Start:** 1 January 2027 or as soon as possible thereafter, no later than 1 April 2027
- **Duration:** 36 months, with possible 12-month extension after positive evaluation.
- **Application deadline:** **1 October 2026**
- **Application contact:** **Prof. Dr. Christoph Bareither**, Ludwig Uhland Institute for Historical and Cultural Anthropology, University of Tübingen.
- **Institutional contact:** christoph.bareither@uni-tuebingen.de; +49 7071 29-2373 (official faculty profile and vacancy).
- **Supervision/team:** The vacancy identifies **Prof. Dr. Christoph Bareither** in digital anthropology/STS and names interdisciplinary collaborators from computer science, psychology and neuroscience. It should be treated as a team-based interdisciplinary supervision environment rather than assuming every named collaborator is a formal supervisor.
- **Eligibility:** Very good master's degree in cultural anthropology/European ethnology or related fields such as STS, sociology, ethnology or media studies; strong ethnographic/qualitative research expertise; excellent English. German is advantageous.
- **Funding:** E13 TV-L, 75%; exact salary amount not stated on the vacancy and therefore **not inferred**.
- **Recent research evidence:** Tübingen lists Bareither's 2026 publication “Hybrid epistemic practices and the transformation of academic assemblages: generative AI and epistemic messiness in the qualitative social sciences and humanities” in *AI & Society*. His university profile also identifies ongoing work on AI-driven transformation of epistemic practices in universities and AI-based survivor testimonies.
- **Research gap / possible PhD directions:** Ethnographic methods for evaluating agentic AI in real scientific workflows; human accountability and division of labour when AI agents perform research tasks; institutional adoption and resistance; epistemic trust and provenance in automated science; effects on researchers' professional identities and practices. These are **analytical research directions**, not university-promised outcomes.
- **Confidence:** **High** for vacancy, deadline, position grade, project scope and institutional contact; **Medium** for formal supervisory arrangements beyond Bareither.
