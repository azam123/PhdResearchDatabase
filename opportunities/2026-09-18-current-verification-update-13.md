### Potential research gaps

These are **database research-gap interpretations**, not claims made by ETH Zurich:

1. **Distributional effects of international tax competition:** connect multinational tax responses with household-level inequality and regional labour-market outcomes.
2. **Tax policy under geopolitical fragmentation:** estimate how changing trade/investment blocs affect profit shifting, tax bases and fiscal competition.
3. **Political economy of fiscal reforms:** combine administrative/electoral data to study when evidence-based tax reforms survive political incentives.
4. **Migration and macroeconomic adjustment:** examine heterogeneous regional effects of migration on productivity, wages and public finances.
5. **Forecasting under structural breaks:** test whether modern econometric methods remain reliable when geopolitical shocks, climate events and policy regimes change rapidly.
