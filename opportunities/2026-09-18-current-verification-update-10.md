## 1. University of Cambridge — MRC Biostatistics Unit: Prioritisation of variables in mixture models for regression and clustering

- **University:** University of Cambridge
- **Unit:** MRC Biostatistics Unit, Faculty of Clinical Medicine
- **Discipline:** Biostatistics, statistics, medical statistics, precision medicine, statistical machine learning
- **Research topic:** Developing methods to prioritise, regulate, or select variables in mixture models for regression and clustering, motivated by clinical applications including osteoarthritis and dementia.
- **Official opportunity:** https://www.mrc-bsu.cam.ac.uk/phd-opportunities
- **PhD programme:** https://www.mrc-bsu.cam.ac.uk/phd-programme
- **Application status:** **Current**; one funded studentship is available for UK applicants for January 2027 commencement.
- **Deadline:** **21 September 2026, 23:59**.
- **Funding:** BSU states that studentships normally provide a generous monthly stipend, tuition fees, and travel/training budget. The current page does not state an exact stipend amount. **Exact amount: unverified/not stated.**
- **Eligibility caveat:** The current funded reopening is specifically for eligible UK applicants; overseas applicants may apply if they have secured their own funding. Do not infer international funding from this listing.
- **Potential supervisors:**
  - Dr Brian Tom — Precision Medicine — brian.tom@mrc-bsu.cam.ac.uk
  - Dr Paul Kirk — Biostatistical Machine Learning — paul.kirk@mrc-bsu.cam.ac.uk
  - Professor Sylvia Richardson — Emeritus Professor/former BSU Director; listed as a supervisor on this project. **Current supervisory availability should be confirmed with BSU.**
- **Official research details:** The project considers weighted likelihoods, adaptive regularisation, informative priors and decision-based/multi-task approaches for variable prioritisation and selection in mixture models.
- **Research gap:** The project description identifies uncertainty over how best to perform variable selection in mixture regression/latent-class settings when variables differ in quality, cost, routine clinical availability, or clinical relevance. A possible research contribution is comparative methodology with principled decision/cost criteria and validation across multimodal clinical datasets. This gap statement is an analytical interpretation, not a quoted university claim.
- **Recent research evidence:** BSU's current Biostatistical Machine Learning theme explicitly focuses on robustness, interpretability, distributional shift and translational biomedical ML. A 2026 Biometrics paper by Sida Chen, Jessica Barrett, Palma, Pan and Brian Tom concerns Bayesian semiparametric modelling of biomarker variability in joint models. These are research-context signals, not claims that the PhD project will reproduce those papers.
- **Confidence:** **High** for current deadline, topic, supervisors and funding structure; **Medium** for project-specific research-gap interpretation; **Exact stipend unverified**.
