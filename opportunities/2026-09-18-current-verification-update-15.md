- **Institutional contact:** hedan.bai@mat.ethz.ch; +41 44 633 29 35
- **Funding:** Official vacancy states a **4-year PhD position, fully funded**, 100%.
- **Timing:** Preferred start as soon as possible in Q3/Q4 2026; applications reviewed on a rolling basis until filled.
- **Eligibility:** The vacancy should be checked directly for the complete qualification requirements before applying; this record does not infer degree equivalence.
