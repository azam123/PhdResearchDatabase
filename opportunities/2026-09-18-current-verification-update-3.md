## Verification and uncertainty policy

- `verified` means the source was checked on **2026-09-18**; it does not mean admission, funding or supervision is guaranteed.
- "Potential supervisor" means the person is institutionally associated with a relevant research area or is explicitly identified by the opportunity. It does not mean they have agreed to supervise a candidate.
- Closed opportunities are not presented as open opportunities. Historical examples may be retained only when they provide useful research/supervisor evidence.
- Institutional emails are included only when published on an official university/institute page.
- Research gaps labelled "database-derived" are analytical suggestions and are **not** claims made by the university or professor.
