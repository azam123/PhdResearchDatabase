## 2. ETH Zurich — Neutral-atom quantum computing

- **Discipline:** Experimental quantum physics, quantum engineering, atomic physics

- **Official opportunity:** https://www.jobs.ethz.ch/job/view/JOPG_ethz_VICQKrWgFLEoyEPTP7
- **Research topic:** Building an ultracold-rubidium neutral-atom quantum processor using optical lattices, high-fidelity collisional interactions, experimental control hardware, numerical/theoretical modelling and quantum-information experiments.
- **Potential supervisor/contact:** Prof. Konrad Viebahn — viebahnk@phys.ethz.ch as listed on the official vacancy. **Note:** the vacancy's displayed address should be checked carefully when applying; use the official application portal rather than email for applications.
- **Deadline:** No fixed deadline; applications reviewed immediately and continue until the position is filled.
- **Target start:** **1 October 2026 or by mutual agreement**.
- **Funding:** 100% fixed-term doctoral position. Exact salary/stipend not stated — **unverified**.
- **Eligibility:** MSc in physics, quantum engineering, electrical engineering or closely related discipline; strong motivation for experimental quantum computing; English.
- **Recent research evidence:** ETH reported in April 2026 that Viebahn and collaborators demonstrated protected neutral-atom SWAP gates with 99.91% fidelity across 17,000 qubit pairs. The group also lists a 2026 preprint on digital programming of spin correlations.
- **Research gaps / possible directions:** scalable collisional gate architectures beyond current demonstrations; site-selective control; integration of high-fidelity gates with error-correction workflows; scaling experimental control while preserving coherence.
- **Verification status:** **High confidence — live official vacancy; funding amount not published.**
