## 4. University of Zurich — forest canopy height and long-term ecosystem change

- **Opportunity:** PhD Position — *Multi-temporal forest canopy height reconstruction from satellite data*.
- **Discipline:** Geography, remote sensing, forestry, climate science, Earth observation, machine learning
- **University / department:** University of Zurich, Department of Geography; collaboration with Department of Mathematical Modeling and Machine Learning
- **Official opportunity:** https://jobs.uzh.ch/job-vacancies/phd-position-multi-temporal-forest-canopy-height-reconstruction-from-satellite-data/f49ee8f9-3652-4f0e-84ab-94c99e374da8
- **Research topic:** Combine multi-source satellite observations, photogrammetry, laser altimetry and machine learning to build a multi-decadal forest structural record from the Alps to the Arctic.
- **Project leads / potential supervisors:** Prof. Livia Piermattei and Prof. Jan Dirk Wegner. The vacancy explicitly states that the project is jointly led by both professors.
- **Institutional contacts:** Livia Piermattei — `livia.piermattei@geo.uzh.ch`; Jan Dirk Wegner — `jandirk.wegner@uzh.ch`. Official UZH pages: https://www.geo.uzh.ch/en/units/rse/staff/piermatteilivia.html and https://www.dsi.uzh.ch/en/people/dsiprofs/jwegner.html
- **Funding:** Funded by the **Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF)** through the project *From Alps to Arctic: Satellite-based Assessment of Forest Canopy Height across Decades*.
- **Start:** 1 January 2027 or by agreement.
- **Deadline:** Applications reviewed from **17 August 2026 and continue until the position is filled**.
- **Recent research evidence:** UZH's Remote Sensing of Environmental Changes group reported the 2025 GlaMBIE work, including the *Nature* paper on global glacier mass changes from 2000–2023, involving Livia Piermattei: https://www.geo.uzh.ch/en/units/rse/news/glambie.html
- **Additional recent group evidence:** UZH reported in 2025 on multi-sensor glacier monitoring and the group's work on open-source environmental remote sensing: https://www.geo.uzh.ch/en/units/rse.html
- **Research gap:** The vacancy identifies limitations of existing large-scale canopy-height products in spatial resolution, temporal coverage and remote/boreal regions. The project directly targets the gap by creating a multi-decadal record across major geographic gradients.
- **Confidence:** **High — official active vacancy, named project leads, institutional contacts and SNSF funding.**

- **Discipline:** Plant biology, microbiology, agroecology, soil science, sustainable agriculture
- **University / department:** University of Zurich, Department of Evolutionary Biology and Environmental Studies; Agroecology & Plant Microbiome Interactions Group / Agroscope
- **Official opportunity:** https://jobs.uzh.ch/job-vacancies/phd-position-sustainable-food-production-with-biologicals/f7d916dc-f51f-4e64-9cdf-e5e8d59c2a56

- **Potential supervisor / contact:** Prof. Dr. Marcel van der Heijden. The vacancy identifies him as the contact for questions; official UZH/Agroscope materials also list `marcel.vanderheijden@agroscope.admin.ch`.

- **Start:** 1 October 2026 or by agreement.
- **Duration:** 4 years.
- **Deadline:** **Not stated; treat as unverified / likely rolling until filled.**
- **Research evidence explicitly cited by UZH vacancy:** Lutz et al. 2023 (*Nature Microbiology*); Anthony et al. 2023 (*PNAS*); Martin & van der Heijden 2024 (*New Phytologist*); Boussageon et al. 2025 (*Journal of Sustainable Agriculture & Environment*); Romero et al. 2025 (*Nature Communications*); Banerjee et al. 2026 (*Nature Reviews Microbiology*); Königer et al. 2026 (*Nature*).
